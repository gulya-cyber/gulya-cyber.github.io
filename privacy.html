Privacy policy

Hermes is a private assistant used only by its owner. This policy describes how it handles Google user data.

Data accessed

With the owner's explicit OAuth consent, Hermes may access the owner's Google Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets and Gmail data, only to perform actions the owner requests.

Use and sharing

Data is used solely to serve the owner's requests. It is not sold, not shared with third parties for advertising, and not used for any other purpose. Hermes's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage

OAuth tokens are stored on the owner's private server. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact
